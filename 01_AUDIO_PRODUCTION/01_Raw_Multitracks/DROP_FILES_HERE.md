# 🎙️ 01_Raw_Multitracks — Drop Raw Live Audio Recordings Here

Drop your unedited multi-track WAV / AIFF / CAF files from the live concert recording here.

### Recommended Naming Convention
- `Track01_Kick_In.wav`, `Track02_Snare_Top.wav`, `Track03_Bass_DI.wav`, etc.
- Or organize by reel/set if recorded in multiple sets (`Set1/`, `Set2/`).

> **Note:** If your raw audio is already saved inside your **Logic Pro (`.logicx`)** package or session folder, you can either keep it inside `02_Logic_Pro_Sessions/` or store shared raw stems here.

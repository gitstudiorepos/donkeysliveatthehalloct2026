# 🔄 02_FCPXML_Interchange — Timeline XML Exports

Export lightweight `.fcpxml` or `.fcpxmld` timeline snapshots here after each editing session (e.g. `Song01_Cut_v1.fcpxml`).

XML exports are tiny, diffable in Git, and let multiple editors exchange cuts or send timelines to Logic Pro / DaVinci Resolve effortlessly.

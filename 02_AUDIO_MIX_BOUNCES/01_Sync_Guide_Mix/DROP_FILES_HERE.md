# ⏱️ 01_Sync_Guide_Mix — Stereo Guide Audio for Multi-Cam Video Sync

Drop full-concert or per-song stereo guide bounces (`.wav` 48kHz / 24-bit) here.

**Why this folder matters:**
Video editors working in **Final Cut Pro** use the audio in this folder as the master audio spine to synchronize all camera angles (`03_VIDEO_CAMERAS/`) into Multi-Cam clips.
